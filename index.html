          Our Process

          Five steps from first meeting to handover. You always know what happens next, who is doing it and what it costs.

          1. 1ConsultationWe visit, measure and listen: needs, style, budget and timeline.
          2. 2Design & 3DLayouts, photo-real 3D views and a walkthrough you see before we build.
          3. 3Materials & quoteChoose finishes at the Experience Centre. Get a clear, itemised quote.
          4. 4ExecutionOur own crew on site, with regular progress updates.
          5. 5HandoverQuality check, deep clean and keys, with after-care when you need it.

          What you receive

          At each stage

          • After consultationA site measurement and a written summary of your brief.
          • After design2D layouts, 3D views of every room and a walkthrough video.
          • Before work startsAn itemised quote and a week-by-week schedule.
          • During executionProgress photos and site visits at agreed milestones.

          Questions

          Before you start

          Ask us anything else in your consultation.

          Do I see the design before work starts?

          Yes. You approve 2D layouts and 3D views of every room, and for full homes we prepare a walkthrough video.

          How long does a project take?

          An apartment typically takes about 40 days. For an independent home, the timeline depends on the total square footage. Either way, we share a week-by-week schedule with your quote.

          How is pricing worked out?

          Every quote is itemised by room and material, so you can see what each choice costs and adjust before you sign.

          Do you offer a warranty?

          Yes, we offer a warranty of up to 10 years. The exact cover depends on the quality of the materials you choose, and we confirm it item by item in your quote.
